# Note to Pallotti faculty (draft)

Subject: See how your students are doing in PathwayEd tutoring

Hi everyone,

Pallotti students can use PathwayEd for tutoring with Nikki, our AI tutor. You can now see how your students are doing there.

**What you will see**

Search for a student by first or last name, or by grade. For each student you can see:

- The skills they are working on, and any they are stuck on
- Their recent wins: skills they have mastered or are ready to move past
- Their skills in Reading, Writing, Math and Test prep
- Their recent activity: which days they worked, in which subjects, and how many check questions they got right

Counselors, administrators and the academic dean will also see a Test Scores tab with placement results and practice test results.

Every number is something the student actually did. Nothing is estimated or predicted. You will not see what students typed to Nikki.

**How to sign in**

1. Go to app.pathwayed.org/staff
2. Choose Continue with Google
3. Sign in with your Pallotti Google account

You will land on the student list. From the Pallotti console, the Tutoring Students link opens the same page in a new tab.

**Good to know**

The page is read only. Nothing you do there changes a student's work, and students cannot see that you looked.

Using it is optional. It is there if it helps you plan or check in with a student.

If you cannot sign in, or something looks wrong, reply to this email and we will sort it out.

Thank you,
David Tenney, Academic Dean
